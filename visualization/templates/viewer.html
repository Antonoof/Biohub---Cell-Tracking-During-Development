Each color is a stable lineage id shared by a cell and its descendants — a sudden color change on a trailing line usually means an identity swap. The three panels are synchronized max-intensity projections of the same frame along each axis, scaled to physical size, so a track that looks fine from the top can still be checked from the front and the side. Trails fade with age. Click a point to highlight just its track and dim the rest — handy when there are too many points to read at once. Scroll to zoom in on a panel, drag to pan, double-click to reset the view. Use ←/→ to step, space to play, m to open the score card.
