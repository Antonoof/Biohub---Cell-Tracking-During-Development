Each color is a stable lineage id shared by a cell and its descendants — a sudden color change on a trailing line usually means an identity swap. The three panels are synchronized max-intensity projections of the same frame along each axis, scaled to physical size, so a track that looks fine from the top can still be checked from the front and the side. Comparing adds a second row of three: the same projections of the other submission, directly below their counterparts. Trails fade with age. Click a point to highlight just its track and dim the rest. Scroll to zoom in on a panel, drag to pan, double-click to reset the view — zoom and pan stay in step across every panel of the same projection, including both rows of a comparison. In the score card, the orange/green chips under “GT divisions” jump to the frame of an annotated division and say why it was or was not recovered. Use ←/→ to step, space to play, m for the score card, s to save, c to compare.
